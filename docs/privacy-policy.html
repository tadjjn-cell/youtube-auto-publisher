Privacy Policy - YT Publisher

Last updated: 2026-09-14

YT Publisher is a personal automation tool built and operated by a single individual to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channel. It is not a public service and does not have external end users.

What it does

The application uses the YouTube Data API (scope: youtube.upload) solely to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, on the owner's own instruction. It does not access, read, or modify any other YouTube account or channel.

Data collected

No data is collected from, or shared with, any third party. The only data processed is: (1) video files the owner personally sends to their own private messaging account, and (2) the resulting video metadata (title, description, tags) generated to accompany the upload. This data is used exclusively to publish the owner's own content to the owner's own channel.

Data sharing

No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. No advertising or analytics services are used.

Contact

For any question about this tool, contact the developer at the support email listed in the associated Google Cloud project.
